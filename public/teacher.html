Presenting from Google Slides? Let phones follow automatically
  1. Download the extension (.zip) and unzip it somewhere permanent.
  2. In Chrome open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and choose the unzipped folder.
  3. Click the extension icon, paste this dashboard link, and click Pair.
  4. Present from Google Slides as usual. Phones follow automatically.
  5. Co-instructors can then move your Google Slides from their dashboard or the phone remote (untick Remote control in the popup to stop that).
