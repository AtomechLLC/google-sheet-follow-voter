Privacy policy

Slide Pulse shows a presentation's slides on students' phones and collects anonymous feedback on each slide. This page explains what this site stores and why.

Instructors who sign in with Google

  • Signing in asks Google for your email address (so signing in again finds your presentations) and read-only access to Google Slides. Slide Pulse cannot edit, create or delete anything in your Google account.
  • It reads only the presentations whose links you paste: the slide list, each slide's text (for slide titles), speaker notes, and slide images, which are stored on this server so students can see the slides without signing in.
  • The access Google grants is stored on this server so slides can be imported again. Signing out discards it. You can also remove Slide Pulse's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • A cookie keeps you signed in on this browser.

Students

  • Students do not sign in. Their phone gets a random ID, kept in the browser, so each person votes once per slide.
  • Stored per presentation: the slide votes ("great", "didn't understand", "question"), the text of any questions typed in, and the instructors' replies. Students should not type personal information into questions.

Instructors and co-instructors

  • The name you type on a dashboard or the phone remote is shown to the other instructors and recorded in the presentation's driving log (who changed the slide, and when).
  • The Chrome extension sends the id of the slide you are presenting to this site so phones can follow along. Its pairing link is stored in your browser.

Translation

If this site has a translation service set up (Google Cloud Translation, DeepL, or Anthropic's Claude), speaker notes and question text are sent to it when someone asks for a translation. Translations are stored with the presentation.

Use of Google user data

Data from Google is used only to show the presentation and its notes to that presentation's audience and instructors. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except the translation service described above. Slide Pulse's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping and deleting data

A presentation's slides, notes, votes, questions and logs are kept until its owner deletes it (Delete on the home page), which removes them from this server.

Contact

Questions about this site's data: contact the instructor who runs it.
