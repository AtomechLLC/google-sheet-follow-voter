Terms of service

Slide Pulse is a classroom tool: instructors show their Google Slides on students' phones and collect anonymous feedback on each slide. By using this site you agree to these terms.

Using Slide Pulse

  • Instructors may only import presentations they are allowed to share with their audience. Anyone with a presentation's student link or code can see its slides and speaker notes.
  • Keep dashboard, co-instructor and phone-remote links private: anyone holding one can control that presentation.
  • Students and instructors must not post unlawful, abusive or personal information in questions or replies. Instructors may delete presentations and their content at any time.

The service

  • Slide Pulse is provided as is, without warranties. It may change, be unavailable, or lose data; keep your original presentations in Google Slides.
  • Access may be removed from anyone who misuses the service.
